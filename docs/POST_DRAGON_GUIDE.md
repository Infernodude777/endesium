# Endesium Post-Dragon Guide

Endesium's content is designed around the moment you defeat the Ender Dragon.
This guide explains what changes after the dragon falls and what there is to
do.

## What changes after the dragon

- The End's outer islands are reorganized into the themed Endesium biomes
  (Wastes, Wilds, Highlands, Marshes, Groves, Expanse, Barrens, Skirts).
- The **Endesium Guidebook** is granted on the first dragon kill, explaining
  the new world.
- Post-dragon events begin; see the `state` package and
  `docs/ENDESIUM_POST_DRAGON_DESIGN.md`.

## The dragon's rewards

Defeating the (post-dragon) dragon yields dragon materials:

- **Resonant Dragon Scale** — used to craft the Resonant Elytra.
- **Dragonbone** — the base material for many high-tier crafts.
- **Dragon Fang** — a rare sharpening component.
- **Dragon Heart** — an epic crafting component.
- **Ender Essence** — a versatile resonance material.

The **Resonant Elytra** (Resonant Wings) is an Elytra wrapped in dragon
materials with passive bonuses — see `ResonantWingsPassives`. It has 864
durability.

## The Void Skirts and beyond

The Void Skirts are the deep edge of the End, home to Void Stalkers, Void
Slate ruins, and the Void metal line. Descending further reaches the **Umbral
Reach** and the **Resonant Archive**, opened with an Archive Key. At the top,
the **Void Crown** holds the dragon arena.

## Suggested post-dragon goals

1. Craft the Resonant Elytra.
2. Build the full Void tool and armor set.
3. Complete the advancement tree, including the new crafting advancements.
4. Wake the Resonant Archive and collect the Archive Sigil.
5. Explore every biome and collect every signature tool.

See `docs/ENDESIUM_DRAGON_AND_CENTRAL_ISLAND_DESIGN.md` and
