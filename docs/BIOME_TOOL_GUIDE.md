# Endesium Biome Tool Guide

Each of Endesium's biomes comes with a signature tool that makes that biome
easier to survive, traverse, or harvest. This guide explains each tool, why it
matters, and how to craft it.

## End Wastes — Wastes Compass

The End Wastes are a flat, featureless plain of Wastes Stone and Wastes
Gravel. Without landmarks, players easily lose their sense of direction. The
**Wastes Compass** keeps a persistent heading toward your last-known home
point, so you can always find your way back to a station or your base.

- Craft: Wastes Stone around a vanilla Compass.
- Use: right-click to set a target, then follow the needle.

## Chorus Wilds — Chorus Pruner

The Chorus Wilds are choked with Elder Chorus Wood, Chorus Root, and Chorus
Moss. Gathering chorus growth by hand is slow and destructive. The **Chorus
Pruner** harvests chorus blocks cleanly and with better yields, letting you
collect Elder Chorus Wood without leaving the forest scarred.

- Craft: two Chorus Eyes on a Stick.
- Use: right-click a chorus block to prune it.

## Shattered Highlands — Highland Grappler

The Shattered Highlands are sheer cliffs of Highland Stone and Highland
Slate. Climbing them the slow way is tedious and dangerous. The **Highland
Grappler** anchors to highland surfaces and pulls you upward, turning
vertical travel into a quick hop.

- Craft: four Highland Feathers and String.
- Use: right-click a highland surface to anchor and pull.

## Void Marshes — Void Filter

The Void Marshes are low-lying wetlands of Void Marsh Soil and Marsh Moss.
Drowning in the murk is a real risk. The **Void Filter** keeps marsh water
out of your breathing, extending underwater survival time in the marshes.

- Craft: Abyssal Thread weave around a Void Membrane.
- Use: equip and enter the water.

## Luminous Groves — Lumen Lantern

The Luminous Groves glow with Lumen Stone, but the light is diffuse and easy
to lose. The **Lumen Lantern** provides a bright, portable light that Lumen
Moths are drawn to, making the groves easier to navigate and the moths easier
to study.

- Craft: Lumen Dust, Void Glass, and a Lantern.
- Use: place or carry for light.

## Ashen Expanse — Ash Sifter

The Ashen Expanse is a desert of Ash Stone and Ashen Soil over hidden lava.
Valuable embers and cinders are buried in the ash. The **Ash Sifter** recovers
Ashen Embers and other cinders from ash blocks, giving the wasteland a
purpose.

- Craft: carved Ash Stone basin.
- Use: right-click an ash block to sift it.

## Crystal Barrens — Crystal Resonator

The Crystal Barrens are full of Crystal Clusters and Crystal Shard Blocks.
Raw crystal is hard to harvest safely. The **Crystal Resonator** tunes the
clusters, making them shatter cleanly and drop more shards.

- Craft: four Crystal Cores around a Void Shard.
- Use: right-click a cluster to resonate it.

## Void Skirts — Void Flare and Void Compass

The Void Skirts are the deep, dark edge of the End. The **Void Flare** marks
your position with a bright signal, and the **Void Compass** (existing)
keeps you oriented. Together they make the dark survivable.

- Craft: gunpowder packed around a Void Shard.
- Use: right-click to launch a flare.

## Everywhere — End Cartographer

The **End Cartographer** tracks the biomes you have visited, building a mental
map of the End's layout. It pairs well with the Echo Compass for planning
long expeditions.

- Craft: Paper and a Compass.
- Use: consult to see explored territory.
