# Endesium Balance Notes

This document records the balance decisions made during the crafting and
progression pass.

## Void metal economy

Void Shards are the entry-level resource, found in the deep End stone. Nine
shards compress into a Void Gem, and a gem smelts into a Void Ingot. This
creates a 9:1:1 ratio from shard to ingot, matching the vanilla iron/gold
nugget economy while keeping Void Ingots meaningfully rarer than iron.

Two alternate ingot paths were added so players are not forced to mine
Voidstone: smelting Voidstone directly (0.8 XP) and blasting Umbral Shards
(0.6 XP, faster). The blasting path is slightly less efficient per input but
much faster, rewarding players who explore the Umbral Reach.

## Utility item costs

The ten biome utility items were priced to be **early-mid game** purchases:

- Items built from biome-native materials (Wastes Compass, Ash Sifter, Chorus
  Pruner, Highland Grappler) cost only those materials, so they are available
  as soon as the player reaches the biome.
- Items that grant stronger traversal or utility (Void Filter, Lumen Lantern,
  Crystal Resonator, Archive Key, Void Flare, End Cartographer) require a mix
  of biome materials and Void metal, gating them slightly later.

## Building block recipes

Building blocks use the standard 2x2 or 3x3 patterns with 1:1 or 4:1 yields,
matching vanilla conventions. Alternate recipes (Void Brick from Umbral Stone
or Voidstone) exist so builders are not locked into a single source.

## Advancement pacing

The new advancement ladder is deliberately linear through the Void metal line
(Shard → Gem → Ingot → Sword) so new players always know the next step. The
biome tool advancements are shallow (single step from the biome entry
advancement) to reward exploration without demanding grind.

## Future tuning

If playtesting shows Void Ingots are too easy to obtain, the Voidstone smelting
recipe can be removed or its XP reduced. If the Resonant Elytra feels too
strong, its durability can be lowered from 864.
