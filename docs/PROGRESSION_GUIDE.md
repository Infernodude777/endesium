# Endesium Progression Guide

Endesium is a post-dragon End expansion. This guide walks a player through the
intended progression from first arrival in the End to the deepest reaches of
the Void Skirts.

## Phase 1 — First steps

After defeating the Ender Dragon, the End changes. New biomes generate around
the central island: the End Wastes, Chorus Wilds, Shattered Highlands, Void
Marshes, Luminous Groves, Ashen Expanse, Crystal Barrens, and the deep Void
Skirts. The Endesium Guidebook is granted on the first dragon kill and explains
the world.

Mine Void Shards from the deep stone. Nine shards compress into a Void Gem,
which smelts into a Void Ingot — the backbone of the Void metal line.

## Phase 2 — Resonance

Craft a Resonance Lens (see the Crafting section below) and use it to activate
ancient Endesium mechanisms scattered through the ruins. Each activation
rewards Resonance Tokens and reveals more of the world's history. The Echo
Compass turns a known signal into a heading, guiding you between stations.

## Phase 3 — Biome tools

Each biome offers a signature tool that makes traversal or gathering easier:

- **End Wastes** — Wastes Compass keeps you oriented in the featureless dust.
- **Chorus Wilds** — Chorus Pruner harvests chorus growth cleanly.
- **Shattered Highlands** — Highland Grappler / Windscar Winch let you scale sheer cliffs.
- **Void Marshes** — Void Filter keeps marsh water out of your lungs.
- **Luminous Groves** — Lumen Lantern provides light that moths follow.
- **Ashen Expanse** — Ash Sifter recovers embers from the ash; Ashwalker Boots let you cross lava.
- **Crystal Barrens** — Crystal Resonator tunes the crystal clusters.
- **Void Skirts** — Void Flare marks your position in the dark; the Void Compass and Void Anchor keep you from getting lost.

## Phase 4 — The Void Skirts

Mine Void Slate and Voidstone, forge Void tools and armor, and descend toward
the Void Crown. The Umbral Reach holds the deepest secrets — and the
Resonant Archive, which can be opened with an Archive Key.

## Phase 5 — Post-dragon mastery

Collect dragon materials (Resonant Dragon Scale, Dragonbone, Dragon Fang,
Dragon Heart) to craft the Resonant Wings and other legendary gear.

# Crafting Reference

Every custom item with a crafting recipe, one entry per recipe. All recipes
are visible in the crafting table recipe book from the start of the game.

## Materials & processing

### Void Ingot
The backbone metal. Two routes:
- Smelt a **Void Gem** in any furnace (0.7 XP, 10 s).
- Or craft from 9 **Void Nuggets** in a 3x3 square.

### Void Nugget
Shapeless: 1 **Void Ingot** → 9 nuggets. Use when you need small change for
compass-style recipes.

### Void Brick (x4)
2x2 of **Void Slate**. Building block line continues below.

### Void Brick Slab (x6)
Row of 3 **Void Bricks**.

### Void Brick Stairs (x4)
Standard stair pattern of **Void Bricks**.

### Void Brick Wall (x6)
Two rows of 3 **Void Bricks**.

### Ashen Crust (x4)
Diamond of **Ash Stone** around an **Ashen Ember**: stone top and bottom,
ember center, stone sides.

## Void gear (netherite tier)

All five tools follow vanilla shapes using **Void Ingot** + sticks:

### Void Sword
Column of 2 ingots over a stick.

### Void Pickaxe
Row of 3 ingots, stick-stick column beneath the middle.

### Void Axe
Ingots in the top-left corner block (II / IS / blank-S shape).

### Void Shovel
1 ingot over a stick column.

### Void Hoe
2 ingots in the top-left corner, stick column below.

### Void Helmet
Vanilla helmet pattern in **Void Ingot**.

### Void Chestplate
Vanilla chestplate pattern in **Void Ingot**.

### Void Leggings
Vanilla leggings pattern in **Void Ingot**.

### Void Boots
Vanilla boots pattern in **Void Ingot**.

## Traversal & utility

### Resonance Lens
4 **Void Shards** in the corners around an **Ender Eye**. Your key to every
resonant mechanism in the ruins.

### Echo Compass
4 **Void Shards** in the corners around a **Resonance Token**. Points at the
nearest known signal once attuned.

### Void Compass
Ring of 4 **Void Ingots** around an **Ender Pearl**.

### Void Anchor
4 **Void Ingots** in the corners around a **Void Core**.

### Void Dash
Ring of 4 **Void Ingots** around a **Phantom Membrane**.

### Void Lantern
Diamond of **Void Glass** around a **Void Ingot** core.

### Lumen Lantern equivalent — Lumen Graft
Diamond of **Lumen Dust** around a **Lumen Graft Block**.

### Ember Charm
Diamond of **Ashen Embers** around **String**.

### Mire Bell Clapper
**Tide Iron** top, iron-string-iron middle, string bottom.

### Windscar Winch
Top row Skyglass Shard–Iron–Skyglass Shard; middle Iron–String–Iron;
iron centered at the bottom. Highlands climbing gear.

## Armor upgrades

### Ashwalker Boots
**Magma Core** on each side of a pair of **Leather Boots**, with an
**Ashen Ember** above each boot slot. Walk lava safely.

## Deep-end items

### Prism Seed
Diamond of **Prism Canopy Block** around a **Chorus Eye**.

### Crown Seal
**Crown Seal Block** corners with a plus of **Ender Essence**.

### Crown Needle
**Crown Needle Block** top, **Void Core** wrapped in **Void Ingots**
beneath it.

### Threshold Key
**Crown Seal** top, **Archive Fragment** center, ring of **Void Cores**.

### Null Quill
Diagonal of **Archive Fragments** with a feather tip and ink sac heart.

### Resonant Wings
Plus-shape of **Resonant Dragon Scales** around an **Elytra** core,
capped by **Ender Essence** on top and **Dragonbone** at the base.
The legendary post-dragon chestpiece.

# Acquisition Reference

How to obtain every custom material, one entry per item.

## Mined

- **Void Shard** — mine the deep End stone under any Endesium region.
- **Void Gem** — nine Void Shards, or found as a rare deep deposit.
- **Void Slate / Voidstone** — surface stone of the Void Skirts.
- **Ash Stone / Ashen Soil** — the Ashen Expanse plains and cone slopes.
- **Crystal Shard Block / Dark Crystal Block** — Crystal Barrens spires and geodes.
- **Tide Iron** — ribs and ruins of the Drowned Cathedral in the Void Marshes.

## Mob drops

- **Ashen Ember** — dropped by Ash Wraiths in the Ashen Expanse; also sifted from ash with an Ash Sifter.
- **Chorus Eye** — Chorus Stalkers in the Chorus Wilds.
- **Lumen Dust** — Lumen Moths in the Luminous Groves.
- **Magma Core** — Blazes in the Great Caldera vault.
- **Skyglass Shard** — Highland storms; found near Windscar caches in the Shattered Highlands.
- **Resonant Dragon Scale / Dragonbone / Dragon Fang / Dragon Heart** — the transformed dragon fight and its rewards.

## Ruins & structures

- **Resonance Token** — activate mechanisms with the Resonance Lens.
- **Archive Fragment** — the Null Archive in the Umbral Reach.
- **Void Core** — flagship vaults; guarded by wardens.
- **Crown Seal Block / Crown Needle Block** — the Crown Observatory tiers.
- **Prism Canopy Block / Lumen Graft Block** — the Lumen Cathedral.

## Crafted from other materials

- **Ender Essence** — condensed from Ender Pearls; used across deep-end recipes.
- **Void Glass** — smelted Void Sand equivalents in the Void Skirts.
- Everything listed in the Crafting Reference above.

## Boss rewards

- **Warden Sigil** — drops from every regional warden, keyed to its region.
- **Golem Core** — the End Golem's drop; the only source, and the reason the
  Effigy exists.
