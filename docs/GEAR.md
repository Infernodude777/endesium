# The Gear Lines - Luminous, Ash, Null, and the Dragon Wings

Three full armor sets with matching tool lines, plus the Dragon Wings - every
one built on the Void set's silhouette but playing as its own material.

## Luminous - the grove's light, worn

- **Full set**: infinite Night Vision + personal glow.
- **Sword**: right-click flashes a prism burst that blinds and marks nearby
  enemies.
- **Pickaxe**: right-click places a lumen lantern (your light-level panel).
- **Axe**: hits drink light, healing you 2 HP per strike.
- **Shovel**: hits grant Speed II for 3 seconds.
- **Hoe**: right-click cleanses blindness, darkness, and wither.
- Crafted from Lumen Dust. Repairs with Lumen Dust.

## Ash - firebending

- **Full set**: permanent fire immunity (the Ashwalker Boots' lava-walking
  still works independently).
- **Sword**: right-click breathes a cone of flame (ignites + damages in a
  6-block arc).
- **Pickaxe**: right-click erupts a burning ring that knocks everything back.
- **Axe / Shovel**: hits ignite for 5 / 3 seconds.
- **Hoe**: right-click wraps you in heat haze (Speed II + fire res, 10s).
- Crafted from Ashen Embers. Repairs with Ashen Embers.

## Null - deletion

- **Full set**: permanent Slow Falling (gravity is optional).
- **Sword**: right-click pulses a gravity well that drags everything toward
  you and applies Weakness II.
- **Pickaxe**: right-click void-steps you 8 blocks forward.
- **Axe**: hits apply Wither I for 4 seconds.
- **Shovel**: hits apply Slowness II for 4 seconds.
- **Hoe**: right-click deletes all your negative effects.
- Crafted from Umbral Shards. Repairs with Umbral Shards.

## Dragon Wings

- Elytra flight (works exactly like the vanilla elytra).
- Iron-chestplate defense (6 armor) baked into the item.
- Only Unbreaking and Mending apply - elytra enchant rules.
- **Permanent Slowness I** while worn: the weight of the dragon's flight.
- Repairs with Resonant Dragon Scales.
- Crafted: 4 Resonant Dragon Scales + 1 Dragon Heart + 1 Resonant Elytra +
  2 Void Shards.

## Where to find them

The four flagship builds (Dust Cathedral, Skyrend Keep, Drowned Cathedral,
Lumen Cathedral) each spawn **named honor-guard minibosses** wearing pieces of
their region's gear. Kill them - the equipment drops at 100%. Each flagship
also hides a **hoard cache** with more gear, dragon materials, and resonance
tokens. Flagships are rarer now: roughly one per 48 chunks in their region.
