# Devlog 17 - The Showpieces

Playtests kept saying the same thing: the fight was fair, the escalation was
readable, but nothing in it made anyone yell. The wisps were pressure; the
titles were flavor. What was missing was a moment - the thing you screenshot.
So the dragon got three set-pieces it performs itself.

## Abyssal Burrow

The dragon folds, plunges below the island, and is simply gone. Invisible,
under the void floor, while the ground rumbles under your feet for three full
seconds. You do not know where it comes back up until the obsidian explodes
underneath you and everything within nine blocks is launched. The counterplay
is honest: when the portal howl sounds, stop standing still.

## Skyward Seize

The meanest thing in the mod. The dragon swoops, takes a player in its claws,
and spirals upward with them for three seconds - they are completely helpless,
hanging off its side while the arena shrinks below. Then it throws them.
Below enrage three the throw is cushioned with slow falling, so it reads as a
brutal repositioning. At enrage three there is no cushion. That is the
difficulty curve the user asked for: same move, meaner contract.

## Gravity Rifts

Four tears in the arena floor that drag everything toward their cores. Not
lethal on their own - but they pull you while the dragon is diving, and the
combination is the point. Rifts only open at enrage three, as the capstone.

## Balance shape

Every special unlocks with enrage and scales on three axes: damage up, cooldown
down, and mercy removed. Burrow at enrage one is a warning shot; at enrage
three it is a killer. The dragon is invulnerable while burrowed and committed
while seizing, so each set-piece trades tempo for spectacle - you get a window
to move, and if you waste it, the move punishes you.
