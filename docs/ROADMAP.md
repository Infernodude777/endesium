# Roadmap

## Done

- Technical foundation and visual design.
- First vertical slice (End Wastes, Resonance, Resonance Lens, End Ruin, Void
  Stalker, First Resonance).
- Geography expansion (End Wastes + Chorus Wilds, transitions, ecology).
- Exploration/structure expansion (ruin variants, Shattered Spire, resonance
  chain, environmental storytelling).
- QA stabilization pass (this milestone).

## Not started (future milestones, planned separately)

- Deep End.
- Additional major biomes.

## Recently completed

- Structure density overhaul: landmark tier (~256-block grid) plus ten
  hand-authored landmarks; flagship vault guards and hazards.
- Mob combat overhaul: regional signature attacks across the ecology,
  Void Wisp (Void Skirts) and Crown Sentinel (Void Crown).
- Bosses: region-adaptive End Warden minibosses in every flagship vault,
  and the End Golem major boss that wakes where the Dragon falls.
- Permanent boss rewards: Warden Sigil attunement (+hearts) and Golem
  Core absorption (+hearts, +damage), with advancements and guidebook
  coverage.

## Scope discipline

The final stop condition for every milestone is: when the current system is
stable, visually distinct, performant, and integrated with the vertical slice,
stop. Do not start the next major feature until it is planned separately.
