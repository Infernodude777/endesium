# Devlog 26 - Boss Polish, and a Fight That Stays Quiet

hey guys.

short one. the boss polish pass I keep promising actually started, and it
turned into a bunch of small fixes that all made the fights *read* better.
nothing shiny, just the stuff that was quietly wrong.

## the sentinel's grab is escapable now

the crown sentinel's grab-and-hurl from a few devlogs ago was a one-way trip.
if it got its hands on you, you watched the animation and then got launched.
no counterplay, which felt wrong for a fight that's supposed to be about
reading the boss.

so it got the same rule the warden and golem already had: **deal enough damage
mid-hold and it drops you early.** 18 damage is the breaking point - enough
that you can't just tickle it, but enough that a player who's actually
fighting back gets out before the throw.

and while I was in there: the goal list had two goals at priority 4, which
meant the sentinel was occasionally picking the wrong thing to do at the same
weight. renumbered so the hierarchy actually means something.

## the fights are quiet now

the dragon and the bosses had picked up a habit of announcing themselves -
big screen titles when phases changed, when it enraged, when scripted attacks
started. i walked through a fight and decided i hated it. the arena already
tells you what's happening. the dragon goes invisible and the ground starts
shaking - you don't need a subtitle to explain that to you. it didn't make
sense for the game to keep narrating its own boss fights.

so all of it is gone. the phase announcements, the enrage titles, the scripted
special intros, the warden and golem grab lines. what's left is what always
should have carried the moment: the sounds, the particles, and the fight
itself. the bosses still get their cues - they just speak through the arena
now instead of a text box.

## next up

the stronghold still hasn't got its advancements - the burrow is found, but
it doesn't have a single achievement attached to finding it. that's next.

the fights are starting to feel like they have a conversation with you now.
one where the boss talks in ground-shaking and portal-lightning, and you get
to answer with a sword.
