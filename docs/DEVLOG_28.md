# Devlog 28 - The Egg Becomes an Altar

hey guys.

the last one. and i want the story to end on the right beat, so let me tell you
what the end of the end actually looks like now.

## the dragon is the entrance, not the exit

i've said it before but it's truer now: killing the dragon is not where the
mod ends, it's where it opens up. the fight itself is a real fight now - the
pillars fight back (crystal aegis heals the dragon while they stand, so you
climb instead of just swinging), enrage is a ladder instead of a switch
(60/35/15 percent, each one wilder, wisps crowding the arena, breath pools
painting the floor), and there are set-pieces that puppet the dragon straight
into the ground under you.

and the respawn is honest now. there was a bug where a respawned dragon would
come back at half health and just... hover. stationary, half-strength, not
doing anything - because it spawned off the wrong end of its own health bar.
fixed. a dragon you raise from a fresh crystal comes in at full health, full
scale, and actually engages instead of floating there judging you.

when it finally goes down you get a hoard worth the trip - dragon wings, a
dragon heart, fangs, bone, resonant scales. the wings alone are the point: real
elytra flight out of the end's own boss.

## the egg is not a trophy anymore

the dragon egg that sits on the fountain after the fight used to be a statue
you could knock around. now it's an altar.

in an older devlog i wrote that the egg hatched a pet dragon. i got that
wrong, and i've fixed it. the egg doesn't hatch ember - she's hers through
other means, and she's still there to be tamed and ridden, three growth
stages, ender pearls and all. no. the egg does something much more important.

the egg summons the end golem.

drop one on the end island and it locks itself down. it won't teleport away
when you right-click it. it won't jump when you punch it. a piston can't shove
it. break the block under it and it won't fall or drop - it's anchored. a
purple boss bar counts down a minute over your head, "End Golem Awakening",
and when the bar empties the egg cracks and the golem rises right where it
stood. every egg you place summons its own golem, and that bar knows when to
leave - it clears the moment the golem spawns, or the second you walk away, or
if the egg is ever moved. no ghost bars haunting you across the end.

and the golem respects the geography better than its predecessor: it won't get
pulled through the exit portal. it walks on top of it. the fight stays in the
end, where it belongs.

## what that means for the endgame

so the progression reads clean now. beat the dragon, take the egg, set it up,
and call up the colossus that guards everything past the first kill. the dragon
fight is the warm-up act for something that doesn't even spawn until you put
the trophy to work.

that's it. that's the end of the end. thanks for following along - go fight
the dragon for real, then come back and raise one of your own.
