# Devlog 27 - The Bosses Hit Back, and You Get a Dragon

hey guys.

big one this time. two things happened and they're both kind of the point of
the mod: the end got *meaner*, and the end finally gave you something to ride.

## the bosses got scary

i went through every boss and rebalanced them around the gear you actually
have by the time you reach them, and the short version is: they hit harder,
they take more, and they stop being pushovers past the halfway mark.

- **the end golem** is now a proper wall. 720 health, 26 damage per swing,
  heavier armor, and once it drops below half it starts repairing itself
  mid-fight, so you can't just chip it down from range.
- **the end warden** got the same treatment - 560 health, 26 damage, armored -
  and it no longer flinches from every little hit. below half it regenerates,
  which means the back half of that fight is a real fight instead of a
  victory lap.
- **the crown sentinel** reforges its plating when it gets low, so the armor
  you cracked open comes back unless you finish it.
- **the void stalker** is harder to knock around, so you can't just juggle it
  with knockback like before.
- and the flagship honor guards all got a pass: more health, more damage,
  more toughness, and resistance so they actually hold a line.

the goal was the same for all of them: the endgame fights should feel like a
wall you climb, not a checkbox you tick.

## the dragon egg is not a trophy anymore

the other big one. that dragon egg sitting on the fountain after the fight?
it's not decoration anymore.

place it back on top of the end fountain and it starts to *work*. ten minutes
later the egg cracks and a baby dragon hatches out - she's called ember, she's
small, and she's not going anywhere.

ember grows through three stages - baby, teen, adult - about five minutes
each. feed her ender pearls and she bonds to you, and once she's an adult you
can hop on.

riding is the fun part. she steers by where you look: WASD to fly, look up to
climb, look down to dive. and if you hold space for three seconds she charges
up and fires a magic ball in the direction she's facing. it's a lot of power
for a mount, but honestly, you earned it.

she's not a vanilla dragon under the hood, either - she won't try to land on
the portal or get hostile, and once she's bonded only you (and the void) can
hurt her, so she's actually safe to bring home.

## the housekeeping

the whole dragon system is wired into the mod properly now - the egg watcher,
the growth, the taming, the ride - so it survives reloads and behaves the
same on client and server. one devlog, as promised, and that's it.

next up i want to give the bosses some new moves instead of just bigger
numbers, but this is a good place to stop and playtest.
