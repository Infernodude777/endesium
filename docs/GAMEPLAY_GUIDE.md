# Gameplay Guide

Endesium adds a quiet layer of discovery to the outer End. Nothing here replaces
vanilla: the Dragon, End Cities, chorus, and Endermen all behave as usual.

## The two biomes

- **End Wastes** — weathered, fractured, sparse End stone with ancient remains.
  This is where you find ruined mechanisms and the Void Stalker.
- **Chorus Wilds** — alive, organic, spreading. Rolling terrain, low chorus
  flora (`chorus_sprout`, `wild_tendril`), and structures partially consumed by
  growth.

The two read clearly differently without the debug screen: dead and fractured
versus alive and growing.

## Items

- **Void Shard** — a fractured remnant. Found in ruin loot and as an uncommon
  independent Void Stalker drop. Scarce by design; not farmable.
- **Resonance Lens** — crafted from Void Shards and an Ender Eye, or obtained as
  the guaranteed drop from a defeated Void Stalker. It reports a qualitative
  resonance reading, not coordinates.
- **Resonance Token** — dropped by every mechanism you wake. It is the proof of
  the discovery and the gate for the Echo Compass.
- **Echo Compass** — crafted from a Token and Void Shards. Turns a signal you
  have already learned to read into a heading and a distance (in tens of
  blocks), with a short white particle trail.

## The loop

1. Explore the outer End until the terrain shifts into Wastes or Wilds.
2. Watch for ruins — small observation stations built from Resonant Slate and
   End Gray.
3. Use the Lens near a ruin. A dormant mechanism will give a faint reading.
4. Activate the mechanism with the Lens to claim its reward (Void Shards and a
   Resonance Token) and earn an advancement. A short white particle line visibly
   travels from the Lens to the mechanism during the wake-up sequence.
5. Craft the **Echo Compass** with your Token and Void Shards, and use it to
   turn the signals you now understand into headings and distances.
6. A stronger, more distant signal now hints at something bigger: the Shattered
   Spire.

## Resonant Blooms

In the Chorus Wilds you will find pale violet flowers with a faint cyan core.
They grow sparsely wherever chorus spreads and glow faintly at night. They are
this milestone's ecological signature — something alive has adapted to the
emptiness and is slowly claiming it back.

## The Shattered Spire

The spire is a rare landmark, larger and rarer than a ruin. It is a place to
look at and wonder about, not a dungeon to clear. Reaching it and waking its
core grants the final discovery advancement.

## After the Dragon

The first time the Ender Dragon dies, the End answers. If you are in the End
you will hear a deep resonance surge, see a contained ring of particles at the
Dragon's death site, and read one line: "The End answers." Everyone else gets a
single quiet system line.

What changed (permanently, for everyone):

- **Dormant mechanisms reach farther.** Signals you could barely feel before
  the fight are now readable at 1.5x distance and strength.
- **The Resonant Archive appears on the Lens.** The loudest signal in the
  wastes is now a sealed domed hall of Resonant Slate and End Stone Bricks,
  older and more intact than any ruin. It was always there; its core only
  wakes now.

Trying to wake an Archive core before the Dragon dies returns:

> The archive is sealed. It waits for the End to wake.

After the transformation, waking the core grants the **Archive Sigil** — the
first post-Dragon token — the `Archive Awakened` advancement, and a fragment
that hints at what sleeps beneath. The transformation never resets: respawning
and re-killing the Dragon does not undo it.

## Mystery is intentional

The mod does not hand out a quest log or waypoints. If you do not immediately
understand a mechanism, experiment: the first interactions are designed to be
understandable through observation.
