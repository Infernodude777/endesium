# Biome Design: Wastes vs Wilds

The two Endesium biomes exist to make the outer End read as a world of distinct
regions rather than one vanilla biome with structures on top. Their contrast is
the point.

## End Wastes — dead, fractured, sparse

- Weathered End stone, fractured ground, shallow fissures.
- Sparse resonant mineral deposits.
- Ancient remains: ruined observation stations and broken mechanisms.
- Quiet emptiness; occasional signs of deeper activity.
- The Void Stalker's preferred biome.

## Chorus Wilds — alive, strange, organic, spreading

- Larger rolling islands, elevated terrain, exposed void gaps.
- Denser chorus growth and low alien flora (`chorus_sprout`, `wild_tendril`).
- Structures partially consumed by growth.
- Softer organic ambience, restrained spores.

## What must not happen

- No dense forest in the Wastes.
- No bright neon purple or fantasy forest in the Wilds.
- No hard line between the two; transitions are noise-driven.

## Contrast levers

| Lever | Wastes | Wilds |
|---|---|---|
| terrain density | low, fractured | rolling, elevated |
| vegetation | none to sparse | denser, organic |
| particles | drifting motes | spores |
| ambience | mineral, sparse | organic, gentle |
| palette | muted, grayed | deeper violet, pale accents |

The two must be distinguishable on sight without the debug screen, while both
remain unmistakably part of Minecraft's End.
