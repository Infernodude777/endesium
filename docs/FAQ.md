# FAQ

## Is Endesium a full End replacement?

No. It extends the vanilla End. The central island, Dragon, gateways, End
Cities, chorus, and Endermen are untouched.

## Are there new bosses or armor?

No. This milestone adds geography, ecology, structures, and discovery. No new
bosses, armor, or post-Dragon progression.

## How do I find End Wastes / Chorus Wilds?

Travel into the outer End. The biomes are noise- and seed-driven; use
`/locate biome endesium:end_wastes` or `endesium:chorus_wilds` to confirm they
are generating, or just fly until the terrain changes.

## How do I get a Void Shard?

Ruin loot and rare Void Stalker drops. They are intentionally not farmable.

## Does the Lens show me where things are?

No. It gives a qualitative reading (faint/strong), never coordinates.

## Is there a quest log?

No. Discovery is environmental. The world itself communicates the chain.
