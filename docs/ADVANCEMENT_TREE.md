# Endesium Advancement Tree

This document maps the full advancement tree of Endesium, including the
pre-existing advancements and the fifteen new ones added in the crafting pass.

## Root branches

The tree hangs off two vanilla roots: `minecraft:end/root` and
`minecraft:story/root`.

### End root branch

- **First Void Shard** *(new)* — obtain a Void Shard.
- **Void Gem Miner** *(new)* — compress shards into a Void Gem.
- **Void Ingot Smith** *(new)* — smelt a Void Ingot.
- **Void Steel** *(new)* — forge a Void Sword.
- **Into the Wastes** — enter the End Wastes.
  - **Waste Scavenger** *(new)* — craft a Wastes Compass.
- **Into the Wilds** — enter the Chorus Wilds.
  - **Wild Tender** *(new)* — craft a Chorus Pruner.
- **Beyond the Edge** — enter the Shattered Highlands.
  - **Highland Hook** *(new)* — craft a Highland Grappler.
- **Below the Surface** — discover the Void Marshes.
- **Follow the Light** — enter the Luminous Groves.
  - **Lumen Beacon** *(new)* — craft a Lumen Lantern.
- **Where Everything Died** — enter the Ashen Expanse.
  - **Ash Prospector** *(new)* — craft an Ash Sifter.
- **Heart of Stone** — discover the Crystal Barrens.
  - **Crystal Tuner** *(new)* — craft a Crystal Resonator.
- **Into the Skirts** — enter the Void Skirts.
  - **A Foothold in the Skirts** *(new)* — mine Void Slate.
  - **Void Signal** *(new)* — craft a Void Flare.
- **The Crown** — reach the Void Crown.
- **The Umbral Reach** — descend into the Umbral Reach.

### Story root branch

- **First Resonance** — activate an ancient mechanism with the Lens.
- **Fractured Station** — activate a fractured station.
- **Sunken Archive** — unearth a buried station.
- **What Remains** — reach the Shattered Spire.
- **The Long Resonance** — follow resonance from ruin to tower.
- **Echo Sight** — craft an Echo Compass.
  - **End Cartographer** *(new)* — craft an End Cartographer.
- **Deep Resonance** — follow a signal to its source.
- **Something Watches** — encounter the Nullwalker.
- **The Scavenger's End** — slay a Dust Crawler.
- **Dragon Transformation** — slay the Dragon.
  - **Dragonbone Carver** *(new)* — obtain Dragonbone.
- **Archive Awakened** — wake the Resonant Archive.
  - **Archive Seeker** *(new)* — craft an Archive Key.
- **Voidforged** — craft a full set of Void armor.
- **Ember Walker** — walk across lava in Ashwalker Boots.
- **Heart of the Volcano** — find a dormant volcano.
